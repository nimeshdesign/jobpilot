Automation behaviour

About auto-submit. Leave it off and JobPilot fills every field, screenshots the finished form and stops so you can check it before submitting. Turning it on submits without review — faster, but a wrong answer to a screening question goes in permanently, and most job boards' terms do not allow automated submission. Your call, your account.

Job sources

Company career boards

Greenhouse and Lever are per-company. These are where visa-sponsoring employers actually post, so this list matters more than any other setting. The token is the company name in the board URL — boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab or jobs.lever.co/aircall. One per line, or comma separated.

Salary expectations by country

The same role is priced differently in Berlin, London and Toronto, so the figure typed into "expected salary" is chosen from the country of each posting and scaled to your experience. These are rough starting estimates, not market data — check them against levels.fyi or Glassdoor and override any that look wrong. Blank means use the built-in estimate.

Standard answers

When a form field's label contains the phrase on the left, JobPilot types the answer on the right. Use it for the recurring questions your field always asks.
